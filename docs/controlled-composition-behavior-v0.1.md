# RC5 controlled behavior

RC5 adds reversible activity execution and independent bounded checking to the
artificial two-member controlled-composition profile. Material construction is
still the separate RC4 capability. Joining both capabilities to a researcher
project and export is RC6; installed and release qualification remain RC7–RC8.

## Original authority and executable meaning

`biocompiler.policy_controlled_behavior_request.v0.1` retains the complete v0.14
request as `original` and adds an explicit executor-owned `readout_evidence`
schedule. Every tick from zero through the original horizon has one ordered
sample, with status and observed tick. Missing evidence has no timestamp;
future observations, authored `stale`, omissions, duplicates and extra fields
reject. Staleness is derived from the original positive freshness bound.
The wrapper changes neither the original source nor the six-input domain.
Readout evidence concerns the modeled activity observation and supplies no
experimental measurement or empirical validation.

The complete original `mechanism_model` and `deterministic_delay` contract are
the executable authority. Their three actual delays are distinct from the five
source deadlines. Semantic definitions referenced by the two product components
remain complete, resolved realization dependencies. Empty component definition
bodies do not become independent executable biological models, and the compiler
does not infer kinetics from them.

The closed candidate graph has eight primitives: encounter state, antigen,
controller, two protein-availability projections, conjunction, a reversible
delay state cell, and a two-band readout. Seven explicit wires connect them.
It retains the complete mechanism, control contract, semantic definitions,
catalog, assembly, context and resource declarations. One executor owns the
controller and persistent activity state. Encounter changes own antigen and
pending activation generation; they cannot clear a committed cessation timer.
RNA presence and protein availability remain separate facts.

## Three independent assessments

The candidate runtime executes only the closed primitive graph and admitted
input batches. An independent reference interpreter uses the original actual
mechanism delays. It imports neither the producer nor the candidate runtime.
Both preserve disabling-before-activation priority, committed cessation despite
restoration, delayed reactivation, and executor state across reset/end.
Actual mechanism delays accept integers from 1 through 10,000, independently
of the at-most-64-tick horizon and source bounds. A transition beyond the horizon
retains its pending state and failed bounded assessment; transport cannot reject
its otherwise valid candidate merely because the delay exceeds the horizon.

The source monitor lowers six original requirements with their complete
identities, source paths and contracts. It reads only the admitted source, the
original input/evidence batches and supplied activity samples. It never reads
actual mechanism delays or invokes either executor. Initial activation and
reactivation accept responses within the requested bounds. Controller, antigen
and component cessation retain independent deadlines: repeated losses cannot
extend them, and one background frame can discharge several. RNA loss alone
does not trigger protein-dependent cessation. Cancellation is applied before a
response deadline is assessed; deadlines are inclusive.

The versioned conservative evidence rule demands readout evidence every tick.
Antigen, controller and both functional protein observations are required while
activity was or is active, the physical gate qualifies, or any response or
cessation obligation is pending. Any unresolved required observation makes the
source assessment unknown, even if later observations recover. RNA evidence is
checked independently on every tick as deployment evidence. The top-level
evidence status concerns demanded evidence and unresolved source assessment:
pending source obligations at the horizon also leave it `unknown`, even when
all observations are valid and a known deadline failure determines the outcome.
Member inventory rows also expose
undemanded protein evidence gaps in completely quiet frames. Unknown evidence
never rewrites a physical input, cancels a physical transition, or removes a
history. The report distinguishes unknown evidence, physical loss and admission
of conditional deployment premises; contradictory original deployment
identities or assumptions fail admission.

## Complete checking and the first composition rule

The checker reparses the complete independent originals and proposal, performs
fresh admission, and reconstructs all candidate bodies and wiring itself. It
imports no producer. Matching a candidate's own hash is insufficient: a fully
repinned graph with a foreign body, port or context still rejects.

The first composition rule projects each member's supplied functional protein
availability into one common gate, then applies one whole-program reversible
activity controller. Local guarantees are identity projections conditional on
supplied delivery/localization premises; they do not assert measured expression
or biological realization. The complete graph is compared against independent
whole-program execution for every admitted history. Isolated component checks
cannot establish acceptance.

Every complete history runs candidate execution, original-model interpretation
and all six source monitors. Reports count every original requirement in every
history, separately report model correspondence and evidence, and retain
member-specific RNA/protein absence and evidence counts. Global acceptance
requires active and background observations, both antigen/controller exclusion
combinations, and a complete background/active/background/active history.
Unresolved obligations or transitions at the horizon, incomplete enumeration,
unknown demanded evidence and vacuous domains grant no capability.

The current fixture has two complete histories, 20 enumerator transitions and
21 prefixes. They share ticks zero and one, branch on controller exposure at
tick two, and retain explicit evidence through tick ten. Both include antigen
and two-member availability loss/recovery; the disabled-controller branch adds
a separate reversible cycle. Independent literal expected bands and adversarial
native tests cover additional timing, evidence and lifecycle cases. The earlier
RC3/RC4 literal bytes and the declarative RC1 acceptance corpus remain intact.
Quiet per-history examples can satisfy source clauses without qualifying a
whole domain's nonvacuity requirement.

## Budgets, replay and boundary

The original work budget owns decoding, production, fresh checking, enumeration,
all execution steps, monitors, retained evidence and final protocol publication.
Domain/history bounds remain explicit; a history is at most 65 completed frames.
Counterexamples retain the first full failing or unknown original history,
including the suffix. No reduction or branch pruning is performed. Cancellation
and exhausted work propagate without a checked capability. A domain exhaustion
or cancellation cannot be relabeled as complete coverage.

Core exposes `construct-policy-controlled-behavior`; Core and independent Verify
expose `check-policy-controlled-behavior` and `replay-policy-controlled-behavior`.
Replay recomputes the complete check before comparing saved evidence. A one-use
publication guard binds the actual executable, operation, request ID and entire
response frame under the same original budget. Python provides immutable input
snapshots and strict transport, without evaluating biological behavior.
Retained physical inputs use `enabled`/`disabled` for the controller and
`present`/`absent` for the other channels. The reader preserves these categorical
values, exact evidence ownership and complete histories, and rejects summaries
that contradict retained requirements, samples, original deadlines or pending
state. Those consistency checks do not independently execute the activity model.

A successful result means conditional modeled behavior and source requirements
hold in the complete declared finite domain. It carries no material/project
export authority, numeric expression or stoichiometric calibration, empirical
function, human-use or clinical conclusion. If real paired inputs require such
quantities, RC2 must acquire their original authority and a versioned contract
and independent checks must precede named-case completion.
