# Two-product, two-member policy composition

This separately versioned implementation extends the policy compiler from one
encoded product/member to two complete RNA members under explicit supplied
component, transport and deployment contracts. It is the first part of priority 4
in the [core implementation plan](core-architecture-session-priorities-2026-10-08.md).
The grounded-helper increment follows separately. Source implementation and
registered witnesses are not evidence of executed validation or release acceptance.

## Bounded source and material contract

The source has exactly two distinct fixed product parameters, each used once by
its own effect, in the existing staged family: one observation, one encounter
machine, five states, seven transitions, two effects and two terminal states.
Every original requirement, finite operating-domain branch, clock and observation
remains authoritative. This profile adds no primitive, effect lifecycle or
observable semantics. The two-observation truth family remains separate.

Each product keeps its own source parameter, graph constant, effect, component
instance, coding feature and RNA-member identity. Binding reconstruction checks
that a source parameter occurrence names only its own actual product output.
Renaming a member or swapping products cannot substitute for that correspondence.

The assembly contains exactly two named instances, each with one complete coding
RNA root, one product and all four required RNA features. Ordered member bindings
associate each instance with its original source root and output member. The
construction emits those two complete roots directly, with no concatenation,
slicing, junctions, complexes or amounts. Local feature identities remain scoped
to their molecule. Independent reconstruction compares every base, feature,
coordinate, origin and chemistry declaration in both output molecules.

Atomic groups remain entirely local to one component. The complete ordered
primitive union retains every node, wire, input, semantic export and group. The
producer uses original source-to-input bindings when arranging components; graph
shape alone cannot choose between source-distinct inputs or products.

## Explicit communication premise

Every cross-member link names its producer and consumer boundary sites and a
pinned transport provider. The provider must be selected by an exact original
implementation-catalog dependency; a proposed link cannot introduce authority.
Its source definition is an interface, and its environment is resolved through
the complete original prerequisite graph.

The sole transport profile is
`biocompiler.policy_complete_signal_identity_transport.v0.1`, under
`biocompiler.policy_staged_primitive_execution.v0.1`. It requires the exact original
clock, zero tick delay, no loss, no duplication, preserved ordering and complete
signal identity. These are supplied model premises. Co-delivery, sequence identity
or an unqualified channel name cannot establish them. Other transport semantics
need their own source/implementation correspondence and preservation contract.

The provider body has schema `biocompiler.policy_material_provider.v0.2` and kind
`transport`, with its original DefinitionRef, recipient, availability,
`capacities: []`, environment, original clock and the fixed transport fields.
The legacy v0.1 provider decoder remains closed; only the explicit new context
admits the new body. The prerequisite graph v0.2 adds a transport-environment edge.
Missing supplied bodies remain Unknown; contradictory identities and contracts
fail. Unsupported premises cannot produce an accepted artifact.

## Deployment and private acceptance

Two original placements bind the complete members to the same declared recipient,
compartment and delivery group. A single checked original delivery contract
supplies the shared arrival, expression, activation and availability window.
Per-member allocation evidence names this shared authority explicitly; staggered
member-specific delivery is outside this profile. Both members and every transport
provider must cover the full original operating interval. Member counts and total
bases include both delivered RNAs. Resource demands remain owned by instances and
are summed against the original shared capacity inventory.

Fresh context checking produces private prerequisite closure v0.2 with complete
member and transport allocations only after all checks pass. Material acceptance
still requires independent source binding, complete finite whole-program
preservation and nonvacuous requirements, complete assembly reconstruction,
context checking and discharge of every original obligation. Saved reports and
caller-supplied closure records cannot create these checked values. Fresh Verify
reconstructs the whole conjunction before paired FASTA/manifest export.

## Independent evidence and remaining scope

The domain-only original fixture retains three exact source inputs: the existing
staged material original and the new literal/support request declarations. Case A
encodes MA and MP; case B retains the first member while changing the second
source product and encoded sequence to MG. Both contain two independently fixed
complete artificial RNA molecules. The original finite domain retains 25 complete
histories, 86 transitions, 87 prefixes and its two hard requirements.

Positive checks must compare the original union, per-product bindings, complete
molecules, carrier and transport projections, closure allocations and exact paired
archives. Negative controls must distinguish source-product swaps, member or
carrier swaps, stale provider pins, missing prerequisites, unsupported transport,
recipient/availability conflicts, capacity overcommit and altered emitted bases.
Source-only, native, SDK, installed/cross-platform and actual-main gates remain
separate and must name the exact revision they exercised.

This profile has zero helpers. A subsequent bounded increment must ground one
helper in an original requirement, independently check its material, bootstrap,
availability and capacity, and count every delivered RNA. Dynamic helper behavior
requires an explicit observation and preservation relation. Supplied molecular
contracts and finite software assurance do not establish actual transport,
expression, therapeutic function or clinical suitability in human cells.
