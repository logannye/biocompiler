# Executable provider prerequisite closure

Implementation checkpoint, 2026-10-08. Native, installed, cross-platform and
actual-main validation are pending. This separately bounded increment implements
priority 2 of the [core architecture plan](core-architecture-session-priorities-2026-10-08.md).
It builds on [named component instances](policy-instance-composition-v0.1.md).

## Contract and acceptance boundary

An implementation catalog may require exact, typed semantic definitions. Those
references become pending obligations during source admission. They do not make
the source executable, grant material acceptance, or establish that a biological
mechanism satisfies its contract.

The new profile retains every catalog dependency occurrence and its owning
catalog entry and original digest. It derives roots from the complete original
deployment, role requirements, delivery declarations and catalog dependencies.
Provider bodies expose only the supported typed dependency relations: interface
environment and chassis capabilities, interfaces and environment. The chassis
operational model identifies the chassis; it is not a self-satisfying dependency.
Traversal retains exact references, provider identities, edges and findings.
It never filters the original operating domain or tests a candidate against an
easier set of assumptions.

The supplied provider inventory must equal the complete reachable inventory.
Every reachable provider is checked against original definition pins, supported
body kind, clock, availability interval, recipient, compartment and original
deployment requirements. Input channels and each instance's resource demand
retain distinct ownership. Sharing one immutable provider body never deduplicates
resource consumption. Shared pools must fund the sum of their reservations.

Missing provider bodies yield `unknown`. Cycles, extra providers, incompatible
bodies, identity conflicts and known resource contradictions yield `fail`.
Uninterpreted contract clauses and unsupported categories cannot discharge a
dependency. Resource exhaustion cannot produce accepted closure or export.
The report retains the graph and any checked allocation prefix on ordinary
negative outcomes; a protocol resource error has no accepted result.

Only a successful complete context check can mint the private
`checked_prerequisite_closure` value. The material coordinator requires that
value and binds its fingerprint into context-dependent obligation discharges.
The final acceptance still requires whole-program bounded preservation,
independent material reconstruction and every original obligation. Fresh export
rechecks the complete original request and candidate. A serialized closure,
saved PASS, matching hash or source admission alone cannot replace that chain.

## Independent witness and checks

The A/B fixture extends the independently authored three-instance witness with
a catalog prerequisite and a distinct transitive environment provider. A separate
catalog-only native control exercises a requirement reachable only through the
catalog. Expected graphs, allocations, molecular output and obligations are
declared independently of the producer and checker.

The fixture retains the 17-base artificial RNA `CCAUGGCUUAAGGAAAA`, the complete
finite domain of 9 histories, 47 transitions and 48 prefixes, and adds one
original semantic-definition obligation for 24 total obligations. The closure
oracle includes 11 root occurrences, 5 reachable providers, 4 dependency edges
and 14 resource-owner allocations. It preserves repeated roots and resource
ownership even when providers are shared.

Distinguishing native controls cover missing, extra, cyclic, wrong-type,
unavailable and wrong-recipient providers; stale pins and candidates;
unsupported clauses and empirical evidence references; resource overdraw and
funded recovery; legacy admission; replay; exhaustion and withheld export.
The source SDK and installed companions retain all 26 lifecycle observations
and both paired archives against the independent original packet. Their source
registration is not execution evidence.

## Deliberate limits

This profile requires nonempty bounded catalog dependencies, named instances,
the existing truth source family, one RNA, one product and no helpers. It closes
supported software contract prerequisites; it does not evaluate arbitrary
predicates, infer conditions from prose, discover mechanisms or implement
dynamic helper initialization. The finite operating domain is unchanged.

The graph permits at most 128 catalog dependencies and 1,024 roots, nodes and
edges each, subject to the enclosing work and output budgets. Model-conditional
acceptance remains distinct from empirical human-cell function and human-use
admission. Source-family and multi-member/helper expansion remain later slices.
