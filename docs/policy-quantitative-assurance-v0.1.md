# Quantitative composition and assurance

This opt-in family connects a bounded quantitative specification to selected
components, their actual executable connections, complete exact RNA construction,
and separately scoped approximation and supplied measurement assessments.
Python authors immutable descriptions. OCaml owns admission, execution,
independent checking and the private capabilities required for export.

## Separately owned state and atomic updates

`policy.quantitative_composition.CoupledTransferNetwork` assigns each reservoir
to an explicit private source `StateStore` and selected component instance. Its
first executable profile admits two to four binary reservoirs on the same exact
substance/unit/quantum, with the existing complete Cartesian bound of sixteen
states. Every store has one owner, and the coordinator is a distinct component.
Unsupported graphs or material arrangements reject explicitly.

Each sample reads one immutable prestate. Directed edges reserve available donor
stock and receiver headroom in their original declared order. Earlier incoming
stock and released room cannot fund another transfer in that sample. Actual
combinational edge signals cross explicit component boundaries; owner fragments
derive their next values from those signals and their own previous values.
The coordinator commits all writes atomically after resource reservation.
Unknown and absent observations hold; encounter reset restores the complete
declared initial vector. Conservation is scoped to accepted samples within an
encounter generation, with reset stated separately.

The independent checker evaluates the actual selected primitives and wiring
over the complete grid. It compares edge allocations, private-state updates,
threshold request labels and commit endpoints to the independent law. Equality
of the final aggregate amount alone is insufficient: a wrong edge, owner,
boundary, reservation order, read source or write target must reject.

The primary artificial example selects four components with three distinct
reservoir owners and one coordinator. It retains the existing exact four-region
single-RNA material profile; one owner also supplies the separately checked
actuator. The mathematical network bound does not promise that every possible
component partition fits that material profile. Larger grids, multiple clocks,
delayed transport and new physical coordination mechanisms remain future
profiles with their own semantics and checking obligations.

## Explicit approximation and finite uncertainty

`policy.approximation` supplies typed `RationalErrorBound`, observation endpoints,
`ParameterInterval`, `ApproximationLink`, `ApproximationChain` and
`ApproximationContract`. Their versioned wire schema is independent of Python
class layout. A chain retains every original intermediate law and observation
map; `.then(...)` proposes a chain and grants no checked evidence.

The first relation compares exact finite network laws under shared True, False,
Unknown and no-update samples, including synchronized reset. It checks every
reachable pair of states at every prefix through the complete material horizon
(`horizon_ticks + 1`, at most 64 samples). Units, nominal substance, sample period,
coordinate order and observation maps are explicit. The bound is an exact
nonnegative rational amount in the complete nominal unit, without floating-point
tolerance or ambient decimal rounding.

Optional uncertainty covers closed quantum-aligned intervals on declared initial
amounts and transfer amounts. Every Cartesian case is enumerated, with a maximum
of eight cases; the nominal case must be included. This is a finite uncertainty
set, not a sampled continuous confidence region. Off-grid or oversized sets
reject instead of silently sampling endpoints. Measured intervals in the
experimental interface below may be off grid, but do not automatically widen
this executable uncertainty domain.

Every visited transition must preserve the exact effect-request label. A small
numeric error cannot justify a different threshold-triggered action. Unknown,
no-update and reset semantics remain visible. Error envelopes retain every
prefix and coordinate. Composition requires exact matching middle endpoints
and scope and sums nonnegative child envelopes under the triangle rule; a later
error cannot cancel an earlier guarantee. The final endpoint must equal the
freshly checked selected component's original mechanism.

New evidence names are `bounded_sampled_observation_error`,
`triangle_error_chain` and
`conditional_approximate_source_material_correspondence`. They supplement the
existing ten exact relations. They neither replace exact source/graph/material
checking nor turn serialized PASS records into private checked capabilities.

The independent nine-state fixture has two reservoirs with capacity two, a
reference transfer amount of one and a target amount of two. Four uncertainty
cases vary initial stock and transfer amount. The maximum coordinate error is
one, while every request label agrees. A second exact link contributes zero
additional error. A separate identity approximation exercises the component
composition profile.

## Supplied experimental realization evidence

`policy.realization_evidence.EvidenceContract` binds each measurement criterion
to the complete selected component pin, instance, mechanism fingerprint and
named transfer parameter. It retains the exact nominal quantity, allowed interval,
replicate requirement and applicability. Applicability binds original recipient,
deployment, clock, operating domain and ordered environment-provider bodies.

A dossier retains complete pinned protocols, datasets and analyses, including
sample period, explicit amount-per-accepted-sample semantics, sample identities,
quantity intervals, artifact digests/locators, origin, producer, timestamps and
analysis software. The checker recomputes the replicate envelope from the raw
supplied intervals and checks the exact dataset/analysis relationship. A claimed
analysis digest alone is insufficient. Quantities must use the complete same
unit; measured intervals need not lie on the executable grid.

The result is `supported`, `incompatible` or `unassessed` **supplied parameter
compatibility**. Missing or insufficient observations, nonapplicable environments
and intervals that straddle a criterion remain unassessed. Contradictory applicable
measurements, stale selected identities or mismatched analyses are incompatible.
Every supplied record and limitation remains in the report.

Synthetic fixtures remain explicitly synthetic. Supplied experimental origin
does not authenticate an external artifact or establish causal validity,
statistical coverage, mechanism function in human cells or clinical benefit.
Those conclusions remain unassessed. Evidence never discharges the formal
component/provider prerequisites or changes an existing material assessment's
`empirical: unassessed` field.

## One complete original request through export

`policy.quantitative_assurance.QuantitativeAssuranceRequest` snapshots the
complete original material request, optional approximation and evidence
contracts, and a bounded supplementary work allowance. Its typed client exposes:

1. `compile`: produce a candidate, then independently check all originals.
2. `check`: independently check the supplied candidate and all originals.
3. `replay`: repeat checking and compare the entire saved result.
4. `export`: repeat checking and emit exact RNA plus the complete manifest.

The verifier cannot compile. An approximation contract, when requested, must
pass to permit export. Experimental compatibility gates export only when the
original evidence contract explicitly sets `require_compatibility=True`.
Otherwise unassessed or incompatible evidence remains visible alongside the
conditional formal result. Supplying a report never creates acceptance.

The new manifest binds the original assurance request, limits, fresh reports and
the unchanged original material manifest and RNA digest. Exact RNA bytes and the
old material manifest remain independently checkable. Each child retains its
own work limits; approximation/evidence work also charges the supplementary
parent allowance. Publication is bounded and checked before returning a result.

This profile deliberately separates three statements: the specification matches
the supplied executable model; the selected material exactly implements the
supplied construction contract; supplied measurements are compatible with a
declared parameter criterion. None alone demonstrates therapeutic function.

## Validation boundary

Pure-Python authoring and transport tests, deterministic fixture reproduction,
strict typing and source inventories are local feedback. Native suites exercise
fresh material compilation, independent checking, replay, export, malformed
authority and semantic counterexamples. Hosted source and installed-package
campaigns exercise the Python facade against actual pinned Core/Verify binaries.
Only the exact hosted run/revision and required integration/main gates establish
their respective acceptance; this document is not a passing validation receipt.
