## Extraction and review procedure

Extraction used Python 3.14 standard-library regex matching of the exact paragraph IDs in the saved HTML. Each of the three sequences was extracted separately. The RNA was not created by converting the DNA; the protein was not generated by translating either nucleotide record. No compiler emitter exists or was used to create expectations.

1. Read protein from paragraph 77, DNA from paragraphs 78 through 91, and RNA from paragraphs 93 through 110. Concatenate paragraph text with a literal newline separator. Stop each selection immediately before its ` (SEQ ID` marker. Preserve this original extraction string in each `raw_sequence_text`, with a separate SHA-256 digest.
2. Remove only ASCII space, tab, CR, LF, vertical tab and form feed; uppercase ASCII sequence letters. Record removal and case-change counts. Reject all other characters, wrong alphabets and ambiguity codes. Preserve the protein's terminal `*`. Do not remove digits, punctuation or OCR errors to make a sequence valid.
3. Visually compare the sequence-bearing description on PDF pages 18–20. The high-resolution PDF, rather than OCR punctuation or letter substitutions, governs a discrepancy. The source records reconcile without sequence corrections.
4. Independently re-extract the retained HTML with a different parser (`HTMLParser` in `tests/test_references.py`) and compare raw strings and frozen hashes. Check T↔U correspondence only after independent extraction.
5. Translate frame zero using [NCBI standard genetic code table 1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Utils/wprintgc.cgi#SG1), require the initial ATG/AUG, preserve exactly one terminal `*`, reject internal stops and incomplete codons. Compare to independently extracted SEQ1.
6. A second reviewer audits source locators, raw/normalized extraction, original PDF and hashes. Record their actual method, date and reviewed source/record identities. An extraction review alone cannot promote a manifest. Accepted imports must retain both passing reviews and no discrepancy.

`independent-audit.json` preserves the second agent’s actual comparison results and reviewed OCR exclusions (printed line numbers/margins and two spurious commas absent from the PDF). Its file hash is pinned and checked on offline load. No nucleotide or amino-acid substitution was made.

This is software-agent source review, not human, experimental or wet-lab validation. Review provenance is not cryptographically signed. Consumers must lock the manifest fingerprint from a trusted copy; changing a record, source identity or review produces a different manifest identity.

## Offline use and storage

`biocompiler.registry.references.load_reference_manifest(path, expected_fingerprint=...)` reads local files, validates the strict schema and checks retained source-file hashes. `manifest.record(reference_id)` rejects candidates and blocked sets by default. Resolution needs no network. Copying this directory to another workspace preserves identities. The original PDF and full HTML are external provenance artifacts; routine resolution uses the retained small source excerpt. Large scans, temporary renders/OCR and generated molecular outputs do not belong in Git.

UTRs, cap, nucleotide modifications, poly(A) tail, full molecule boundaries/topology, regulatory context and exact manufactured material identity are unresolved. An accepted fixture supplies scoped source identity/structural consistency only; the molecular behavior contract remains unestablished.
